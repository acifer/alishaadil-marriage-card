The Celebrations, a guide for our guests. Three moments on one day, Saturday 28 November 2026, Patna: I, The Nikah, Sat 28 Nov, 4 PM, Kishan Palace, Qubool, the vow; II, Reception Dinner, Sat 28 Nov, 8 PM, Kishan Palace, Ziyafat, the feast; III, The Rukhsati, Sat 28 Nov, after dinner, Dua, the blessing.

What each moment means. Nikah, the marriage contract: The marriage itself. Before witnesses, bride and groom each say Qubool hai, “I accept”, sign the contract, and the gathering raises its hands in prayer. Reception, the wedding feast: Both families gather at long tables for a celebratory dinner, to greet the newly married couple and share in their joy. Do come and say hello. Rukhsati, the bride’s farewell: Alisha leaves her parents’ home to begin her new life, sent off with prayers and rose petals. A tender, often tearful moment; your blessings mean everything. Words you may hear: Mubarak, congratulations. Dua, a blessing. Shukriya, thank you.
